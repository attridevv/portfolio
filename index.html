In the Gray Between Dreams

Oh shades of light, show me the way, Through darkness that swallows and night that turns gray. When I can't seem to breathe, let me dance in the dawn, And find in the shadows the strength to go on. In a pocket of dreams and the depths of my wants, I still find myself crawling back through the sands. Yet in each grain of hope, a new light I can flaunt, As I rise with the dawn, where my courage still stands. What I seek is not freedom, as seeking confines, And what I like is not love, but a flicker that shines. For I like what is light, just a glimmer to find, In the maze of the night, in the folds of my mind. White is not always warm, and black not always stark, There's a darkness that's white, there's a whiteness in dark. In the contrast we find all the shades in between, In the gray of our dreams, all our truths can be seen.

Life Happened

Life happened, me waiting. Believing it would stop, it would wait. Life happening and happening, Consistent like spring water. Oh water, don't reflect what I see. Once five, now twenty—what changed? What changed? The vessel grew wings, then they were cut, And strings were pulled. Cut these strings before they strangle me, Drag me to somewhere the mirror guides. What happened to the wings that used to carry the train? Was the wind too strong, or were the strings too tight? Either way, the reflection doesn't lie. Oh water, let's start again. Guide me, let's start again. Take me away to the far sea, Where I can sleep once again.
