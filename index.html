the mind

questions i sit with

What is the meaning of life?
The meaning is emotion. The highest highs, the lowest lows — that's why we're here. To feel everything. Joy, grief, love, heartbreak, adrenaline, stillness. If you die having felt it all, you lived right.

When did we stop asking questions?
When asking stopped being rewarded. School teaches answers, not curiosity. Society likes boxes.

Why does being drunk make us happy?
Because it quiets the voice. The one that worries about tomorrow, judges every move, keeps us small. The real question: why do we need relief from ourselves?
